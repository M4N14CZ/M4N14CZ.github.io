Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

SupoClip Publisher is a single-operator automation tool. It uses the YouTube API only to manage the operator's own YouTube channel (BlowMyMind): uploading video files and their titles, descriptions and metadata that the operator prepares.

We do not collect, store, process, share or sell any third-party user data. No analytics, cookies or trackers are used on this site. YouTube API data touched by the tool belongs exclusively to the channel owner.
